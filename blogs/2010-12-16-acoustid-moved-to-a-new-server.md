Since I announced the Acoustid project, I got over 1.1 million fingerprint submissions (mostly from MusicBrainz editors ), covering about 580 thousand unique MusicBrainz track IDs. In the background I was running a process that imported the raw submissions and merged similar tracks. All this was done on a virtual machine with 1GB of RAM.
